06

Case study: Germany and Erbil

An example of why operational context matters when interpreting annual application totals.

—change in German applications processed in Erbil, 2024→2025

Most of Germany's overall decline in applications recorded in Iraq was concentrated in Erbil. The decrease coincided with the temporary cancellation of appointments at the German Visa Application Centre in Erbil from 22 June 2025 until further notice.

Interpretation: the timing is consistent with an operational disruption affecting application volume, but annual aggregate statistics cannot establish that the suspension was the sole cause.

07

Data & methodology

Definitions, limitations and downloadable data used in the explorer.

Coverage

Short-stay Schengen visa statistics reported for consular posts located in Baghdad and Erbil, 2015–2025.

Unit of analysis

Applications and decisions are classified by consular post and destination state. They do not represent applicant nationality.

Cases not issued share

Cases not issued ÷ recorded decisions. “Not issued” should not automatically be treated as identical to a refusal in every statistical context.

MEV share

Multiple-entry uniform visas issued ÷ total uniform visas issued. LTV visas are not included in this calculation.

What this explorer can — and cannot — tell us

It can describe trends in application volume, recorded decision outcomes, destination concentration and consular workload. It cannot identify the probability that a particular applicant will receive a visa, establish causal effects from annual correlations, or show current appointment availability unless that information is separately verified.
